# common/coat_of_arms/coat_of_arms is a replace_paths folder, so vanilla's copy of
# this file does NOT load. Bespoke content per board item #3629 — the 11
# Nehekhara country tags (common/country_definitions/07_nehekhara.txt, same order).
# Composed entirely from vanilla gfx/coat_of_arms/ art (patterns + colored_emblems,
# neither blanked by replace_paths) plus one Realms of Exether emblem (disclosed
# below), and this mod's named_colors palette (common/named_colors/00_coa_colors.txt
# + 01_wh_coa_colors.txt). Requires common/flag_definitions/07_nehekhara.txt to
# actually render in-game (see that file's header, and 02_empire.txt's for why).
#
# Two disclosed substitutions (no literal match exists in vanilla or the RoE set):
#   - KHM: the tomb-city's pyramid is stood in for by ce_triangle.dds (a plain
#     triangle charge), paired with ce_cobra_coiled.dds for the royal uraeus.
#   - MAH: the City of the Dead's skull motif is stood in for by a voided
#     (black-on-black) ce_sun.dds — a dead, lightless sun rather than a skull charge.
# One vendored asset: ZND uses ce_roe_crocodile.dds from the sibling Realms of
# Exether mod (reuse grant 2026-09-07) — see the provenance comment on its block.
