# Agro-climate overrides (draft of the model note's section 5)

Status: draft for #3677, built with `mapgen/config/state_climate_classes.csv` and `mapgen/config/canon_rivers.csv`. The model note `planning/economics/agro_climate_model.md` absorbs this file as its section 5 after Chris's verdict on the class render. `override_ref` in the class CSV points at the entry ids below.

How to read it:

- Lore assigns each state's class; `mapgen/tabulate_state_prior.py` flags a class the physical prior does not expect (`phys_disagree`) and hard-errors unless the row carries an entry id. Entries marked with a bracketed reason after a state (`[band]`, `[highland]`, `[not_highland]`, `[no_canon_river]`, `[not_coastal]`) are those disagreements; the rest are band-direction or subsistence entries, including every case the workspec names.
- Direction vocabulary: `class:<slug>` (the class departs from the physical expectation), `up` / `down` (position in the class band), `subsistence:<building id>`.
- Magnitude is in band steps from the class default `mid`: 1 is `low` or `high`; 2 leaves the class band (a written entry is what #3676 section 5 requires for that) and the realm child sets the value below the class p25, or above its p75.
- Sources are Lexicanum pages read 2026-10-04, cited as page, section and the GW publication the page footnotes. The reasons are paraphrases; nothing here is a quotation.
- Citation tiers as in the class CSV: A zone extract by name or hub, or a canon name that states the land type; B realm-wide extract, or a zone reached only through the state's owner or worksheet source; C nothing in the extracts (keeps the physically expected class, carries no entry); T Total War name.

Counts at authoring: 343 states, 49 `phys_disagree` (all carry an entry), 82 `heat_disagree` (non-blocking, answered in the notes below), 42 entries.

## Realm-level readings behind the class map (for section 4, not overrides)

- **Norsca.** Cold, forested and mountainous, with few viable arable fields worked only in the warmer half of the year; the people live off the sea, the hunt and some farming. Coast-named states take `arctic_boreal_coast`, mountain-named and highland-flagged states `highland_alpine`, the rest `boreal_forest`. Zone-level text exists only for the Skaeling coast, the Bjornlings, Kraka Drak and Hell Pit.
- **Kislev.** Steppe, not forest: the oblast is open frozen grassland; the north shades into Troll Country tundra, the east is more wooded toward the mountain foothills, and farming is only viable in the south. Realm-level text for most states; zone-level for Troll Country and the oblast.
- **The Empire.** Zone-level text for every province. River plains where the lore gives fertile farmland on a named river (Reikland, the Upper Reik around Nuln, Averland, the Moot); forest provinces (`temperate_forest_hill` with forest caps) for Middenland, Nordland, Hochland and Talabecland; Ostermark's Veldt is grassland (`steppe`); Sylvania and the Wasteland are ruined land.
- **Bretonnia.** Zone-level text for every dukedom (Knights of the Grail through Lexicanum). Wheat strips, vineyards and orchards realm-wide; Aquitaine, L'Anguille and Bordeleaux are the arable heartlands; the Marches of Couronne are dry plains (`steppe`); Mousillon, the Grave of Cuileux, Montfort and Carcassonne are poor. Athel Loren is forest, not farmland, and is reached only through owner tags.
- **Karaz Ankor.** Good farming land is scarce in the mountains, dwarfs are poor farmers and keen hunters, and grain and fruit come in by trade; surface works do include goat-herding stations and farms. Every mountain state is `highland_alpine`. Zone-level land-use text exists only for Karak Eight Peaks, the Black Water, Black Fire Pass, the Black Mountains, the Irranas, the Apuccini and the Sour Sea; most pages carry no land-use text and the class rests on the state's name.
- **Southern Realms.** Estalia is warm and dry (fruit, oil, wine) and Tilea farms grain, vines, olives and sheep: `mediterranean`. The Border Princes are petty settlements of mixed farming, cattle, timber and sheep: `temperate_forest_hill` (Balkan analogue), realm-level only.
- **Badlands.** Arid, rocky, boulder-strewn steppe and marsh with thorn scrub, once more fertile (a settled kingdom, Strygos, stood on the Blind River): `steppe` at realm level, position `low` for the realm child. The Marshes of Madness are the one wet zone.
- **Nehekhara.** A desert land. The Great Mortis strip was Nile-grade farmland in life and is poisoned now, except around Numas.
- **Dark Lands.** Almost nothing can grow: `open_desert` realm-wide, with zone-level text for the named wastes (Howling Wastes, Wolf Lands, Zorn Uzkul, Plain of Zharr, Desolation of Azgorh, Plain of Bones) and one named exception, Gnoblar Country. The ogre holds, the dragon coast, Five Rivers, Haunted Grove and the Wheatlands have no lore and keep the physical class.
